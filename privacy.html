Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This application is a personal-use tool that connects to a single QuickBooks Online company owned and operated by the developer. It accesses accounting data (such as invoices, customers, bills, and reports) solely for the developer's own internal use.

This application does not collect, store, sell, or share data with any third party. No data is used for advertising or analytics. All data accessed via the QuickBooks Online API remains within the developer's own systems.

This application is not distributed to or used by any other end users.
